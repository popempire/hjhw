*Music as Dream* from *Upheavals of Thought* by Martha Nussbaum

*Really the Blues?* by Allen Lowe

I dreamed recently that someone was had cut down the great oak trees on the Western
side of my parents' house, the one large one at the corner of their property 
and the "three sisters" where the paper street ran. I came upon the scene just as 
they were finishing cutting down the one large one. I don't know who it was that
was cutting them down — they were not hired by my parents, as far as I understood.
I immediately became furious and tried to confront them verbally. I granted that
they probably had a right to cut down the three trees on city property, but objected
strongly against their cutting down the one on my parents' land.
